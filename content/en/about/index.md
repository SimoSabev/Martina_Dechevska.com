Martina began her journalism career at just 18, contributing to Pod Tepeto, a local website in her hometown of Plovdiv, before earning a bachelor's degree in Journalism from Sofia University, majoring in Radio and International Journalism. Her early experience included the Bulgarian News Agency (BTA), where she stood out as a top-performing intern, and — between the ages of 19 and 21 — Dow Jones Factiva, covering financial markets, the opening and closing of the Paris Stock Exchange, and international mergers and acquisitions.

## New York

New York marked a defining chapter. Martina began contributing to VICE, Maxim and Dnevnik.bg, developing a voice that moves between contemporary culture, art and international affairs. Her television career began with BuntART, the cult arts and culture programme on Bulgarian National Television, where she held a dual-role contract covering both on-camera reporting and production behind the camera.

She went on to become a US correspondent for bTV, one of Bulgaria's leading television networks — from the United Nations General Assembly and interviews with presidents to major political and cultural stories — and later established the New York bureau of ZoomIn.TV, the Amsterdam-based international video news platform, expanding into entertainment, cinema and fashion. Her work has taken her from intimate conversations with emerging artists to red-carpet interviews with Richard Gere, Julianne Moore, Ryan Reynolds and Gal Gadot.

## Writing

Long before New York, Martina built a devoted readership in Bulgaria with her blog *A Wish for Wings That Work*. In New York she founded Flowers in a Gun, an independent music blog named after The Libertines' "The Boys in the Band", leading a collective of writers and photographers documenting the city's independent music scene. Her debut novel, *S@MOT@*, was published in 2015; she has since completed an English-language version and is finishing her next novel, written in English. She continues to write on fashion and culture for the Mexican magazine Saltillo 360 and works independently as a freelance journalist and cinematographer.

## Real estate

Today Martina is a Licensed Associate Real Estate Broker and the Founder and Managing Director of The Empire Team at Find Real Estate, representing buyers, sellers, landlords and investors across New York City. Within her first six months in the industry she secured exclusive representation of 12 Manhattan buildings, and she has spent more than a decade mentoring agents. Beyond the city, she owns and runs Windham Art House in the Catskills.

Fluent in Bulgarian, English, French and Spanish, Martina approaches every story — and every client — with the perspective of someone who has lived between cultures, disciplines and worlds.
