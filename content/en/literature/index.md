Literature has always been at the heart of Martina's creative identity. In 2008, while in Tunisia, she began writing *S@MOT@* (Samota), a debut novel that took seven years to complete and appeared in 2015 as a provocative departure from the conventions of contemporary Bulgarian fiction.

Drawing on interviews she conducted with women exploring BDSM, Martina turned journalistic material into a dark, genre-bending narrative where cyberpunk, psychological suspense, erotic fiction and anime-inspired aesthetics converge. Set against a world of social networks and fractured intimacy, *S@MOT@* follows two young women trapped in very different forms of captivity, exploring desire, power, identity and the unsettling influence of the digital world.

To protect her creative vision, Martina assembled an independent editorial and launch team, working with Teo Chepilov, a writer on the Bulgarian television series *Undercover*, and cover artist Alexandra Ramirez. On its release the novel drew national media attention — ELLE reported that it was being called Bulgaria's answer to *Fifty Shades of Grey* — and reached No. 4 on Helikon's December 2015 e-book chart. It was presented at literary events in Sofia and Plovdiv with Avgustin Gospodinov, Nikoleta Parusheva and Aleksandar Sekulov.

## The next chapter

Now based in New York, Martina has completed an English-language version of *S@MOT@* and is pursuing its publication while finalising her next novel, written in English. Her fiction continues to explore the fault lines between intimacy and isolation, freedom and obsession, and the private worlds people build to survive.
